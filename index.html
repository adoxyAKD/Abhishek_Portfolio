// Who I am

About

I'm a Flutter & Android Developer based in Gurgaon, with 4.5+ years building mobile apps that make it all the way to production — not just working demos. Built on a strong native Android (Kotlin) foundation, the last 2.3+ years have been spent shipping production Flutter/Dart apps — from a ride-hailing platform live across two countries to a full native-to-Flutter migration.

At Ahead Websoft Technologies, I led a full native-to-Flutter migration of a social networking app — rebuilding its UI, navigation and core functionality from scratch while keeping every existing feature intact, then shipping it live on both the Play Store and App Store. Before that, at I-Next Web Technologies, I owned HappyTask end-to-end, a service booking platform still live today.

I care about the parts of mobile development that don't show up in a screenshot: clean MVVM architecture, tests that actually catch regressions, and a release process that doesn't break at 11pm.
